About this prototype

A conversational agent for Kenya Airways customer support, built on a fine-tuned BERT language model.

The project

Kenya Airways proposed the concept of a virtual assistant that handles routine customer questions (flight status, baggage, check-in and booking changes) so contact-centre agents can focus on complex cases. This prototype was designed and built to that brief and will be presented to Kenya Airways at the end of the project.

Passengers can ask in natural language, by text or voice. The assistant understands the request, keeps track of the conversation over several turns, carries out simple transactions with a confirmation step, and hands over to a human agent when needed. Staff can review conversations and escalations in a dashboard.

All flights, bookings and accounts in this prototype are sample data. Policy details such as baggage allowances are illustrative and would be confirmed with Kenya Airways.
