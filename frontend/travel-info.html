No single bag may weigh more than 32 kg; heavier items travel as cargo. Extra and overweight bags can be bought in advance. Allowances vary by route and fare, so always check your e-ticket.

Check-in

  1. Online, from 36 hours before departure Check in on the website and get your boarding pass.
  2. At the airport, from 3 hours before Counters open three hours before departure.
  3. Bring your documents Your booking reference and a valid passport or ID.

Frequently asked questions

Can I carry a bag that weighs more than 32 kg?

No. For safety, no single checked bag may weigh more than 32 kg. Split the contents across bags or send heavier items as cargo.

How do I change the date of my flight?

Ask the Virtual Assistant or use My bookings. You'll see the current and new flight before anything changes, and nothing is updated until you confirm.

Can I cancel my booking online?

Yes, confirmed bookings can be cancelled through the Virtual Assistant. Refunds are processed within 7 business days. Bookings that are already checked in need an agent.

What if my flight is delayed or cancelled?

Check the latest status on the Flight status page or ask the assistant. If your flight is cancelled, the assistant can move your booking to another date or connect you to an agent.

How do I reach a person?

Type "agent" in the Virtual Assistant at any time, or call +254 20 327 4747.

Still have a question?

The Virtual Assistant knows the baggage and check-in rules and can look up your booking.
