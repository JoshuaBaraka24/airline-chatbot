Meet the Kenya Airways Virtual Assistant

Ask in your own words, by typing or speaking. The assistant understands what you need, looks up your flight or booking, and gets it done, with a person always one message away.

What it can do

Tap an example to try it. The assistant opens with your question already asked.

Flight status

Live departure and arrival times, delays and cancellations, with advice on what to do next.

Change a booking

Moves your booking to another date. It shows the current and new flight and waits for your confirmation.

Cancel a booking

Cancels confirmed bookings after showing you exactly which flight will be cancelled.

Find a flight

Searches the schedule for your route and dates and lists the flights with seats available.

Check-in

Tells you whether you're checked in and exactly when online check-in opens for your flight.

Baggage questions

Checked and cabin allowances, overweight bags and what to do with heavy items.

How it works

  1. Ask in your own words Type or tap the microphone. No menus or keywords needed.
  2. It understands what you need A language model trained on airline questions picks out your request, flight number, booking reference and dates.
  3. It checks before acting It looks up your flight or booking and asks you to confirm before changing anything.
  4. A person is one message away Type "agent" at any time, or the assistant hands over if it can't help.

Built for everyone

  • Speak your question and have answers read aloud
  • Works fully with a keyboard and screen readers
  • High-contrast mode from the header or the chat window
  • Logged in? It offers your own bookings, so there are no references to type
